Who We Are

Our History & Community

Newcomb & District Cricket Club has been a community cornerstone in east Geelong since 1972, competing in the Geelong Cricket Association (GCA). We run senior men's, senior women's, and junior programs out of Grinter Reserve, 141 Coppards Road, Moolap.

The club proudly holds Good Sports accreditation, reflecting our commitment to a safe, responsible, and supportive sporting environment for members, families, and volunteers.

We share a long-standing community bond with the Newcomb Power Football Netball Club, our neighbours at Grinter Reserve, working together to serve the local sporting community across seasons.
