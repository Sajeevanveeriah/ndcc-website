Newcomb & District Cricket Club

Providing inclusive community sport and elite cricket development at Grinter Reserve.

Our History and Community

Newcomb & District Cricket Club is proudly affiliated with the Geelong Cricket Association and continues to develop players in a competitive and welcoming club environment.

The club is accredited through Good Sports, reinforcing a strong commitment to a healthy, safe, and inclusive sporting culture for players, families, and supporters.

At Grinter Reserve, Newcomb & District Cricket Club shares deep community roots with the Newcomb Power Football Netball Club, building year-round local connection through sport.

World Class Facilities

The Peter 'Skinny' Harrison Training Facility opened in August 2024 and provides high-quality cricket training access for the wider community and NDCC programs.

3 Public Synthetic Lanes

Accessible synthetic training lanes that support local participation and regular skill development sessions.

4 Club Reserved Turf Lanes

Dedicated turf lanes reserved for club use, enabling structured training aligned to elite cricket development.
