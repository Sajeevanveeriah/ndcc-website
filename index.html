Our History and Community

The Newcomb and District Cricket Club is a cornerstone of community sport in the Geelong region. Affiliated with the Geelong Cricket Association and Cricket Victoria, we offer opportunities for players of all ages and skill levels.

Known proudly as the Dinos, we share a rich local history with the Newcomb Power Football Netball Club. Our mission extends beyond the boundary rope, focusing on social engagement, cultural diversity, and partnering with organizations like the Newcomb Softball club to ensure inclusive participation.

Training Facility

Opened August 2024

Officially opened in August 2024, our new training complex at Grinter Reserve represents a massive investment in local sports infrastructure. Named in honor of our legendary player and long-time curator, this facility replaces outdated nets to provide a centralized, safe, and inclusive environment for all members.
